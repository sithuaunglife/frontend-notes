# AI Career Readiness

## Facts
- CTF Prompting Formula:
 - CTF = Context + Task + Format
 - The CTF formula is a prompting technique for giving AI clear instructions.
 - Context: Provide the background information AI needs to understand the situation.
 - Task: Explain what you want AI to do.
 - Format: Specify how you want AI to present the output.
 - Providing these three elements helps AI generate more relevant and structured responses.
- Protecting Personal Information When Using AI:
 - Avoid sharing sensitive or confidential information with AI tools.
 - Personal information: Don't share sensitive personal details.
 - Organizational identity: Avoid disclosing your company's name or identifying details. If necessary, describe the general business context without revealing its identity.
 - Credentials: Never share passwords, API keys, authentication tokens, or other access credentials.
 - Financial information: Don't disclose sensitive financial details or account information.
 - Confidential company information: Don't upload internal documents or proprietary data unless the organization has explicitly authorized the use of the AI tool for that purpose.
 - Key principle: Give AI only the information necessary to complete the task, and remove sensitive details whenever possible.
- Some websites offer LinkedIn profile assessments or optimization scores.
- LinkedIn is more than a job-searching platform. It can also help you build your professional reputation, network and personal branding.
- Use a clear, professional-looking profile picture to help people recognize you and create a positive first impression.
- The banner is the background image displayed at the top of your LinkedIn profile.
 - It can communicate your professional identity and reinforce your personal brand.
 - You can include:
 - Your professional role or specialization.
 - Your key skills or value proposition.
 - A portfolio website or other professional link.
 - Contact information, if appropriate and safe to share.
 - Think of the banner as a digital business card that visually communicates who you are and what you do.
- LinkedIn Headline Formula
 - Formula:
 - [Role] + [Value / Strength] + [Audience / Context]
- Role: Your professional title or target role.
- Value / Strength: What are you good at? What skills or strengths do you offer?
- Audience / Context: Who do you help, or what field are you focused on?
- About Me Section Formula
 - Formula:
 - [Role] + [Value / Strength] + [Audience / Context]
- Treat AI as a senior mentor who guides you and provides professional advice.
- Format: Specify how you want AI to structure its response, such as bullet points, notes, or tables.
- Job titles are inconsistent. Search by job responsibilities and required skills, not just job titles.
- Job Alerts: Turning on LinkedIn Job Alerts is important. Without them, searching for jobs becomes tiring and requires manual effort.
- Top Applicant: LinkedIn's Top Applicant feature is worth paying attention to when searching for jobs.
- Job Match: Check how well a job matches your LinkedIn profile before applying.
- Resume Scanners: Resume scanner websites can help evaluate your CV.
- Self-Learning Projects: If you lack professional experience, include self-learning projects and small experiences from work you have done.
- AAR Framework — Action, Achievement, Result: A framework used to structure CV content.
- PAR Framework — Present, Achievement, Reason: A framework used to structure CV content and cover letters.
- Cover letters are becoming increasingly important in recruitment.
- AI as a Thinking Partner: AI is a thinking partner, not the final decision-maker.
- Company Research: Check Indeed and Glassdoor for company reviews, read employee comments, and review the recruiter's LinkedIn presence before applying.
- Questions to answer when checking a company:
 - 1. Does the company culture feel healthy?
 - 2. Is the workload realistic?
 - 3. Are employees leaving bad reviews?
 - 4. Is the salary appropriate for your location?
 - 5. Does this role actually match your long-term career goals?
- STAR Framework — Situation, Task, Action, Result: A framework used in interviews to structure answers about past experiences.
- AI Interview Practice: Use an AI prompt to practice answering interview questions using the STAR framework.
- Voice Practice: When practicing interviews with AI, speak using the microphone instead of typing. This helps simulate a real interview, where you need to communicate your answers verbally.

## Syntax
**Career Assistance Setup Prompt**
```txt
[COPY > PASTE > EDIT]
Career assistant instructions
You are my personal career assistant. My CV is attached. Always refer to it
before you answer anything about my background, skills, or job fit.

About me:
Name: [your full name]
Experience level: [fresh graduate / 1-2 years / career switcher]
Target role: [e.g. Marketing Coordinator, Data Analyst]
Target country and city: [e.g. Yangon, Myanmar]
Industry I want to work in: [e.g. tech startup, healthcare, government]
Languages I speak: [e.g. English, Burmese]
What I am open to: [full-time / internship / remote / hybrid]

When I paste a job posting, always do this:
1. Tell me how well my CV matches this role. Give a percentage and
explain why.
2. Tell me exactly what to change in my CV to match better.
3. List the keywords I should add to my CV from this posting.
4. Tell me if anything in the posting looks like a warning sign.
5. Tell me honestly if I should apply or look for a better match.

Always end every answer with one clear next action I should take.
```
- A setup prompt for creating a personal AI career assistant that refers to my CV, evaluates job matches, recommends CV improvements, identifies relevant keywords and potential red flags, and advises whether I should apply for a position. It also ensures that every response ends with one clear next action.

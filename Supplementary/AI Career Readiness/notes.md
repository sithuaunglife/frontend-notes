# AI Career Readiness

## Facts
- CTF Prompting Formula:
 - CTF = Context + Task + Format
 - The CTF formula is a prompting technique for giving AI clear instructions.
 - Context: Provide the background information AI needs to understand the situation.
 - Task: Explain what you want AI to do.
 - Format: Specify how you want AI to present the output.
 - Providing these three elements helps AI generate more relevant and structured responses.
- Protecting Personal Information When Using AI:
 - Avoid sharing sensitive or confidential information with AI tools.
 - Personal information: Don't share sensitive personal details.
 - Organizational identity: Avoid disclosing your company's name or identifying details. If necessary, describe the general business context without revealing its identity.
 - Credentials: Never share passwords, API keys, authentication tokens, or other access credentials.
 - Financial information: Don't disclose sensitive financial details or account information.
 - Confidential company information: Don't upload internal documents or proprietary data unless the organization has explicitly authorized the use of the AI tool for that purpose.
 - Key principle: Give AI only the information necessary to complete the task, and remove sensitive details whenever possible.
- Some websites offer LinkedIn profile assessments or optimization scores.
- LinkedIn is more than a job-searching platform. It can also help you build your professional reputation, network and personal branding.
- Use a clear, professional-looking profile picture to help people recognize you and create a positive first impression.
- The banner is the background image displayed at the top of your LinkedIn profile.
 - It can communicate your professional identity and reinforce your personal brand.
 - You can include:
 - Your professional role or specialization.
 - Your key skills or value proposition.
 - A portfolio website or other professional link.
 - Contact information, if appropriate and safe to share.
 - Think of the banner as a digital business card that visually communicates who you are and what you do.
- LinkedIn Headline Formula
 - Formula:
 - [Role] + [Value / Strength] + [Audience / Context]
- Role: Your professional title or target role.
- Value / Strength: What are you good at? What skills or strengths do you offer?
- Audience / Context: Who do you help, or what field are you focused on?
- About Me Section Formula
